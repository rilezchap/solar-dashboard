Key Assumptions:
- Electricity prices are based on the `state_prices` dictionary, which reflects average rates per state.
- The cost per watt for solar installation is assumed to be $2.5.
- The Investment Tax Credit (ITC) rate is set at 30%.
- Annual electricity generation is estimated at 1500 kWh per kW of installed capacity.
- O&M (Operations and Maintenance) costs are assumed to be $15 per kW annually.
- Electricity prices are assumed to increase by 2% annually.
- The financial model spans a 25-year period, which is the typical lifespan of a solar system.

Financial Model Structure:
1. **Input Parameters**: User inputs including state and roof size (kW) are collected via the Streamlit interface.
2. **Cost Calculations**: The system cost is calculated based on the roof size and cost per watt. ITC is applied to reduce the upfront cost.
3. **Revenue Calculations**: Annual revenues are calculated based on electricity prices, generation capacity, and price escalation.
4. **O&M Costs**: Annual O&M costs are computed based on the roof size.
5. **Cash Flow Generation**: A cash flow table is generated by combining revenues, O&M costs, and upfront costs.
6. **Key Metrics**: IRR and payback period are calculated to evaluate the financial viability of the solar system.

Technical Choices:
- **Streamlit**: Chosen for its simplicity and ability to create interactive dashboards quickly.
- **Pandas**: Used for tabular data manipulation and display of cash flow tables.
- **NumPy Financial**: Utilized for financial calculations such as IRR.
- **Modular Design**: The financial model is split into reusable functions in `financial_model.py` for better maintainability and testing.
- **Scalability**: The architecture allows for easy updates, such as adding more states or adjusting assumptions.
